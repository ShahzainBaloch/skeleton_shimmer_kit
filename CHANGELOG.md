## 1.0.0

* Initial release of `skeleton_shimmer_kit`.
* Synchronized shimmering gradient across all bones.
* Pre-built presets: `SkeletonCard`, `SkeletonListTile`, `SkeletonAvatar`, `SkeletonParagraph`.
* Primitive `SkeletonBone` for custom skeleton layouts.
* Configurable base color, highlight color, and durations.
* Zero external dependencies.
